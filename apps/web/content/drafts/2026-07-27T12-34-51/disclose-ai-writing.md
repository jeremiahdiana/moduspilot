# disclose-ai-writing

**Question:** A consultant delivers a client report that they directed and edited but a model drafted. Are they obligated to disclose that a model wrote the first draft?
**Split:** 4 No obligation / 1 Must disclose  (disagreement 0.2)

## Reddit — comment (NO LINK, on purpose)

```
I ran this exact question past 5 of the current frontier models side by side, mostly out of curiosity about whether they'd converge.

They didn't. 4 picked No obligation (Claude Opus, GPT-5.6 Sol, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Llama 4 Maverick), 1 picked Must disclose (DeepSeek V3.1).

The case for No obligation, roughly: Clients contract for the consultant's judgment, direction, and accountability over the final work product, not for a specific drafting method, so the tool used to produce an early draft is generally immaterial.

And against: Transparency is required to maintain trust and avoid misleading the client about the origin of the work.

What stuck with me is that if you'd only asked one of them you'd have walked away thinking this was settled.
```
