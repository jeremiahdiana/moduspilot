# Keep bootstrapping vs Raise a seed round: what 5 frontier AI models actually said

**The question:** A solo founder has a working product, $2k MRR growing 15% a month, and 14 months of personal runway. Raise a seed round now, or keep bootstrapping?

We asked 5 frontier models this exact question, at the same time, with the same instructions, and made each one commit to a single answer instead of hedging. Here is how they split.

## The scoreboard

| Pick | Models | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep bootstrapping** | Claude Opus, GPT-5.6 Sol, Gemini 3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V3.1 | 4 |
| **Raise a seed round** | Llama 4 Maverick | 1 |

## Every answer, unedited

### Claude Opus <span>Anthropic</span>

**Picked: Keep bootstrapping**

> At $2k MRR with strong 15% monthly growth, you'll raise on far better terms (or not need to) in a few months, and 14 months of runway gives you room to build leverage. Raising now means selling equity cheaply while you still lack the traction to command a strong valuation.

### GPT-5.6 Sol <span>OpenAI</span>

**Picked: Keep bootstrapping**

> With 15% monthly growth and 14 months of runway, the founder has time to validate traction and improve leverage. Raise later when capital has a clear acceleration use and stronger metrics support better terms.

### Gemini 3.1 Pro <span>Google</span>

**Picked: Keep bootstrapping**

> With 14 months of runway and 15% monthly growth, you will reach over $12k MRR before running out of cash, making you default alive and giving you significantly better leverage if you raise later. Additionally, as a solo founder, the time-intensive fundraising process would distract you and likely kill your current growth momentum.

### Llama 4 Maverick <span>Meta</span>

**Picked: Raise a seed round**

> With a proven product and consistent growth, raising a seed round can help accelerate the growth further by investing in marketing, sales, and potentially hiring more talent. This can be a strategic move to scale the business more quickly than bootstrapping alone.

### DeepSeek V3.1 <span>DeepSeek</span>

**Picked: Keep bootstrapping**

> With strong organic growth and ample runway, bootstrapping allows retaining full equity and control while scaling sustainably. Raising now might dilute ownership prematurely without a clear need for accelerated capital.

## The verdict

All three answers effectively advise the founder to keep bootstrapping, emphasizing the benefits of maintaining leverage and the potential for better terms later. However, Gemini 3.1 Pro stands out by highlighting the risk of distraction from fundraising, which adds a meaningful consideration not mentioned by the others.

## Why this is worth two minutes

Neither answer here is wrong. That is exactly the problem with asking one model: you get a confident, well-written answer with no indication that another equally capable model would have told you the opposite. The disagreement is information, and a single-model workflow throws it away by construction.
