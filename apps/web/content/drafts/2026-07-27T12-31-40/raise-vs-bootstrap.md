## Reddit — comment (NO LINK, on purpose)

```
I ran this exact question past 5 of the current frontier models side by side, mostly out of curiosity about whether they'd converge.

They didn't. 4 picked Keep bootstrapping (Claude Opus, GPT-5.6 Sol, Gemini 3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V3.1), 1 picked Raise a seed round (Llama 4 Maverick).

The case for Keep bootstrapping, roughly: At $2k MRR with strong 15% monthly growth, you'll raise on far better terms (or not need to) in a few months, and 14 months of runway gives you room to build leverage.

And against: With a proven product and consistent growth, raising a seed round can help accelerate the growth further by investing in marketing, sales, and potentially hiring more talent.

What stuck with me is that if you'd only asked one of them you'd have walked away thinking this was settled.
```
