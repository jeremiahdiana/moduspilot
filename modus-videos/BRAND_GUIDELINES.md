# MODUS Pilot Brand Guidelines for Video Creation

## Visual Identity

### Primary Color Palette

| Element | Color Code | RGB | Use Case |
|---------|-----------|-----|----------|
| Brand Purple | #7C3AED | 124, 62, 237 | Primary CTAs, logos, key accents |
| Light Accent | #a78bfa | 167, 139, 250 | Secondary elements, highlights |
| Dark Variant | #6d28d9 | 109, 40, 217 | Hover states, emphasis |

### Accent Gradient

Used for animated headlines, button reveals, and dynamic CTAs:

```
#a78bfa → #7c3aed → #c084fc → #818cf8 → #7c3aed → #a78bfa
```

### Background & Text Colors

#### Light Theme (Default)
- **Background**: #FAF9FF (RGB: 250, 249, 255) — Off-white, cool tint
- **Panel/Surface**: #FFFFFF (white) — Card surfaces, modals
- **Border**: #E2DCF4 (RGB: 226, 220, 244) — Light purple-tinted dividers
- **Text (Primary)**: #0D0D14 (RGB: 13, 13, 20) — Near-black, high contrast
- **Text (Muted)**: #645A7A (RGB: 100, 90, 122) — Secondary text, captions

#### Dark Theme
- **Background**: #0A0A0F (RGB: 10, 10, 15) — Near-black
- **Panel**: #111118 (RGB: 17, 17, 24) — Dark surfaces
- **Text**: #E8E8F0 (RGB: 232, 232, 240) — Off-white

### Logo & Brand Mark

- **MODUS LOGO.png**: High-resolution brand mark in deep purple
- **Sizing**: Minimum 100px width for visibility
- **Clear space**: Maintain padding equal to 25% of logo width
- **Backgrounds**: Works on light backgrounds, light text on dark
- **Variations**: Full logo, mark-only, horizontal layout

## Visual Storytelling

### Video Structure Templates

**Problem-Solution-CTA** (App Store, Founder Pitch)
1. Hook with problem (2-3 sec)
2. Establish context (5-8 sec)
3. Introduce MODUS (5 sec)
4. Show key features (15-20 sec)
5. State benefits (10-15 sec)
6. CTA (2-3 sec)

**Hook-Feature-CTA** (Social Ads)
1. Grab attention (2-3 sec)
2. Problem statement (3-4 sec)
3. Reveal solution (3-4 sec)
4. Key benefit (3-4 sec)
5. CTA (2-3 sec)

**Education-Explanation-Action** (Explainer)
1. Hook: Why this matters (5 sec)
2. Context: The problem (10-15 sec)
3. Solution: How MODUS works (20-30 sec)
4. Benefits: Why it matters (10-15 sec)
5. CTA: What's next (5 sec)

### Visual Principles

1. **Purple as Guide, Not Dominator**
   - Use purple for 15-25% of the frame
   - Accent key moments (CTAs, logo reveals)
   - Don't overwhelm with purple — let it guide attention

2. **Minimalism is Strength**
   - Clean backgrounds
   - Clear typography
   - Breathing room between elements
   - No unnecessary animations

3. **Real Over Rendered**
   - Show actual MODUS interface
   - Real screenshots over mockups
   - Authentic interactions over CGI
   - Your genuine voice over polish

4. **Contrast for Clarity**
   - High contrast text on backgrounds
   - Clear visual hierarchy
   - Obvious focal points
   - Color (purple) draws the eye

5. **Motion that Communicates**
   - Transitions show relationships
   - Animations reveal information
   - Speed matches energy level
   - Purposeful, not decorative

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### TikTok & Instagram Reels

- **Vertical format**: 9:16 aspect ratio
- **Hook timing**: 0-1 second critical
- **Pacing**: Cut every 2-3 seconds
- **Audio**: Trending sounds, captions for sound-off
- **Tone**: Creator-authentic, conversational
- **Purple usage**: Accent, not dominant
- **CTAs**: Subtle but clear (bio link, website)

### App Store

- **Landscape format**: 1080p (16:9)
- **Pacing**: Slower, more breathing room (5-7 sec per scene)
- **Focus**: Product showcase, not brand story
- **Quality**: Professional, polished, premium feel
- **CTA**: "Download Now" or "Coming Soon" overlay
- **Purple usage**: Consistent throughout, guides attention

### Website & YouTube

- **Format**: 1280×800 or 1080p landscape
- **Pacing**: Moderate (4-6 sec per scene)
- **Structure**: Clear intro, body, conclusion
- **Audio**: High-quality narration, professional background music
- **Tone**: Educational, authoritative, trustworthy
- **Purple usage**: Branding consistent, not overdone

### Podcasts (Internal/Thought Leadership)

- **Format**: Landscape 1080p
- **Focus**: Conversation, not production
- **Audio**: Native dialogue with natural flow
- **Tone**: Peer-to-peer, knowledgeable, engaging
- **Visuals**: Minimal, background supports dialogue
- **Purple usage**: Subtle branding, doesn't distract

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
✓ Lead with your differentiation (OS vs. chatbot)
✓ Show real MODUS interfaces and features
✓ Use your authentic voice and personality
✓ Create multiple variations and test performance
✓ Build series and recurring content
✓ Match video style to platform norms
✓ Maintain brand consistency across all videos

### Don'ts
✗ Don't compare feature-for-feature with other AI
✗ Don't hide your purple branding
✗ Don't use overly produced, corporate content
✗ Don't create generic "AI tool" messaging
✗ Don't ignore platform-specific best practices
✗ Don't sacrifice clarity for creativity
✗ Don't be timid about your differentiation

## Measurement & Iteration

### Metrics to Track

1. **Engagement**
   - View completion rate
   - Click-through rate
   - Share rate
   - Comments and replies

2. **Performance**
   - Watch time
   - Retention curve (where do viewers drop off?)
   - Traffic driven to site
   - Conversions (sign-ups, demos, downloads)

3. **Brand Lift**
   - Brand awareness
   - Message recall
   - Perception of differentiation
   - Likelihood to try/recommend

### Iteration Process

1. **Create variations** (3-5 of the same concept, different angles)
2. **Publish and measure** (track engagement metrics)
3. **Identify winners** (which angles, messages, pacing perform best?)
4. **Double down** (create more content in the winning style)
5. **Refresh and repeat** (keep testing, don't get stale)

## Questions to Ask Yourself

**Before Publishing**
- Does this video lead with the problem MODUS solves?
- Is the differentiation clear (OS vs. chatbot)?
- Does the purple branding feel intentional, not forced?
- Would my team be proud to share this?
- Does it match the platform's best practices?
- Is the CTA clear and easy to follow?

**After Publishing**
- What worked? What didn't?
- Which scenes drove the most engagement?
- Where did viewers drop off?
- What message resonated most?
- How does this compare to my best-performing content?
