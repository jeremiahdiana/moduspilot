# MODUS App Teaser Generator

Create professional App Store teaser videos that showcase MODUS Pilot as a personal AI OS. This skill produces 1080p cinematic videos from your app screenshots with your MODUS branding and key message: **"Why pay for a chatbot when you can have a whole system?"**

## Brand Guidelines for Your Video

- **Primary Color**: #7C3AED (MODUS violet)
- **Accent Color**: #a78bfa (lighter violet)
- **Style**: Professional, minimalist, creative
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds
- **Format**: 1080p (optimized for App Store)
- **Key Message**: "Your personal AI operating system"

## How to Use

1. **Gather App Screenshots**: Select 4-5 high-quality screenshots from MODUS showing:
   - The AI in action (email tracking, calendar integration, etc.)
   - Core capabilities (personal knowledge, habit tracking)
   - User benefits (time savings, intelligence)
   - Social proof or success metrics
   - Call-to-action screen

2. **Describe Your Focus**:
   - What's the main hook? (e.g., "AI that knows your life")
   - What problem does MODUS solve? (Replace chatbots with a full OS)
   - What's the desired action? (Download, learn more)

3. **Customization Options**:
   - **Music**: Professional, modern tech soundtrack
   - **Pacing**: Builds from problem → solution → call-to-action
   - **Color Treatment**: Your screenshots will be subtly enhanced with MODUS violet accents
   - **Branding**: Includes MODUS logo and tagline overlay

## Output

A cinematic 1080p MP4 video ready to upload to App Store Connect, with:
- Beat-driven editing
- Professional transitions
- MODUS branding applied consistently
- "COMING SOON" or "DOWNLOAD NOW" overlay (your choice)

## Tips for Best Results

- **Screenshot Quality**: Use bright, clear UI screenshots showing real app features
- **Variety**: Mix close-ups (showing detail) with full-screen views
- **Action**: Prioritize screens showing your AI actively working
- **Messaging**: Be specific about what makes MODUS different (it's an OS, not a chatbot)
- **Brand Consistency**: All screenshots should feel cohesive in your app design

## Next Steps

1. Have your App Store screenshots ready
2. Write a brief description of what you want to emphasize
3. Run this skill and upload your screenshots
4. Get back a ready-to-submit App Store video

Your app teaser will position MODUS Pilot as the intelligent operating system alternative to traditional chatbots.
