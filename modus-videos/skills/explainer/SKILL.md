# MODUS Explainer Video Creator

Generate 60-80 second explainer videos that walk viewers through MODUS Pilot, your features, or specific use cases. Perfect for your product page, onboarding docs, or educational content.

## Brand Guidelines for Your Video

- **Primary Color**: #7C3AED (MODUS violet)
- **Style**: Professional, clear, minimalist
- **Duration**: 60-80 seconds
- **Format**: 1280×800 desktop view with avatar
- **Key Message**: MODUS is your personal AI operating system
- **Tone**: Knowledgeable, approachable, straightforward

## How to Use

1. **Choose What to Explain**:
   - Overall MODUS capabilities and positioning
   - Core features (email tracking, calendar integration, habit learning)
   - User workflows (how MODUS works behind the scenes)
   - Problem/solution (why MODUS replaces traditional AI)
   - Use case demonstrations

2. **Prepare Your URL or Topic**:
   - If walking through your website: have the URL ready
   - If explaining a concept: write a clear 1-2 sentence description
   - Provide context on what viewers should understand by the end

3. **Script Your Explanation**:
   Keep it structured:
   - **Opening** (5-10s): What is MODUS?
   - **Problem** (10-15s): Why you need it
   - **Solution** (20-30s): How MODUS works
   - **Benefits** (10-15s): What makes it different
   - **CTA** (5-10s): What's next?

## Output

A professional 1280×800 video with:
- Real browser walkthrough of your product or website
- Animated avatar host (on-screen guide)
- Professional background and transitions
- MODUS branding and color accents
- Clear, engaging narration with lip-sync

## Content Ideas

**For Product Pages**:
- Feature overview: "MODUS learns your habits"
- Integration showcase: "Connects to your email, calendar, devices"
- Differentiation: "Operating system, not a chatbot"

**For Onboarding**:
- Getting started with MODUS
- How your personal AI OS learns about you
- Privacy and data handling explanation

**For Marketing**:
- Why AI OS > Chatbots (your positioning)
- Day-in-the-life with MODUS
- Competitive comparison

**For Educational Content**:
- How AI can be personal
- Privacy-first AI operating systems
- The future of human-AI collaboration

## Tips for Best Results

- **Clarity First**: Assume the viewer knows nothing about MODUS
- **Show, Don't Tell**: Use real interface demos and interactions
- **Keep Pace**: Slow enough to follow, fast enough to hold attention
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Let your purple accents guide focus
- **Examples**: Concrete use cases beat abstract features
- **Authenticity**: Your avatar guide should feel like a knowledgeable friend

## Where to Use

- **Product page**: Hero explainer video
- **Documentation**: Feature walkthroughs
- **Email campaigns**: Onboarding sequences
- **Social media**: LinkedIn, YouTube, blog posts
- **Sales decks**: Pre-call education

The explainer video is your chance to educate viewers on what makes MODUS different: it's not just smarter AI, it's an operating system that knows you.
