# MODUS Founder Pitch Video Creator

Generate professional founder-style pitch videos (60+ seconds) perfect for investor presentations, product announcements, or public speaking engagements. Features a talking founder with real product screenshots and MODUS branding.

## Brand Guidelines for Your Video

- **Primary Color**: #7C3AED (MODUS violet)
- **Style**: Professional, confident, authoritative
- **Duration**: 60-65 seconds
- **Format**: 1080p (16:9 landscape)
- **Key Message**: MODUS is the personal AI operating system
- **Tone**: Vision-driven, passionate, clear

## How to Use

1. **Prepare Your Pitch**:
   - Write a 60-90 second script (not too fast, ~150-170 words)
   - Structure: Problem → Vision → Solution → Why Now → CTA
   - Focus on differentiation: OS vs. Chatbot

   **Example Structure**:
   - "Everyone's asking for smarter AI, but..."
   - "What if AI could actually know you?"
   - "Introducing MODUS Pilot, your personal AI OS..."
   - "It learns your habits, connects to your life..."
   - "The future isn't smarter chatbots, it's operating systems"
   - "Join us at [site/demo]"

2. **Gather Product Screenshots**:
   - 4-5 real, high-quality screenshots of MODUS in action
   - Show core features (email integration, calendar, habits)
   - Mix UI close-ups with full-screen views
   - Keep them on-brand with your purple accents

3. **Optional: Avatar Setup**:
   - Choose a founder persona (or use a generic tech founder)
   - Select tone/presentation style
   - Customize intro/outro messages

## Output

A professional 1080p video with:
- 4 × 15-second acts of a talking founder
- Your real MODUS product screenshots on the founder's phone
- Professional transitions and background
- MODUS branding and logo
- 5-second branded end card with CTA

## Script Framework

**Act 1: The Hook (Problem)** (0-15s)
"Everyone's obsessed with AI, right? But every tool we get is the same: you type something, you get a response, and that's it. It's still a chatbot, just with more parameters."

**Act 2: The Vision** (15-30s)
"What if AI could actually be part of your operating system? What if it learned how you work, tracked your emails, your calendar, your habits, and actually became useful?"

**Act 3: The Solution** (30-45s)
"That's MODUS Pilot. It's not another chatbot. It's your personal AI OS that runs for you, knows you, and actually connects to your life."

**Act 4: The Call-to-Action** (45-60s)
"The future isn't smarter models. It's smarter systems. Check out MODUS Pilot at [yoursite.com]. See how AI should actually work."

## Tips for Maximum Impact

- **Energy**: Conviction over casual — this is your vision
- **Specificity**: "Connects to your email and calendar" beats "integrates with your tools"
- **Contradiction**: Lean into the problem you're solving
- **Visual Proof**: Let your screenshots do the talking — they're real
- **Simplicity**: One big idea, not five features
- **Purple Presence**: Use your brand color subtly but consistently
- **Authenticity**: Your voice and personality matter

## Where to Use

- **Investor pitches**: Send before meetings for context
- **Product announcements**: Launch emails, social media
- **Speaking engagements**: Conference intros, talks
- **Press/media**: Newsworthy stories and coverage
- **Marketing funnels**: High-intent audience content

## Length Variations

- **60s**: Full pitch (ideal for most uses)
- **30s**: Short teaser (social media, ads)
- **90s**: Extended narrative (detailed explainer)

This is your chance to embody your vision: MODUS isn't about being smarter, it's about being different. You're not building the next chatbot, you're building the OS it should have been.
