# MODUS Social Ad Creator

Generate short-form vertical video ads (15-60s) optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Create compelling creator-style product reveals with your MODUS brand colors and key message.

## Brand Guidelines for Your Video

- **Primary Color**: #7C3AED (MODUS violet)
- **Style**: Minimalist, energetic, authentic creator voice
- **Duration**: 15-60 seconds (vertical 9:16)
- **Format**: TikTok/Reels native
- **Hook**: "Why pay for a chatbot when you can have a whole system?"
- **Tone**: Conversational, relatable, slightly irreverent

## How to Use

1. **Choose Your Angle**:
   - **Pain Point Hook**: Start with the problem (chatbots are limited)
   - **Reveal Style**: Unbox or demo the solution (your AI features)
   - **Comparison**: Show what's wrong with other tools
   - **Success Story**: Quick win or use case demonstration

2. **Script Your Message**:
   Example hook: "tired of chatbots that forget everything? Meet MODUS Pilot..."
   
   Keep it:
   - Personal and authentic
   - Problem-focused (not feature-focused)
   - Action-oriented (what can the viewer do?)
   - 1 main idea per video

3. **Customize the Ad**:
   - **Visual Style**: Minimalist with MODUS purple accents
   - **Music**: Trending audio or professional background track
   - **Pacing**: Fast cuts, energetic transitions
   - **CTA**: Clear call-to-action (visit site, download, learn more)

## Output

A 1080p vertical video (9:16) with:
- POV creator-style talking head (animated)
- 3-4 jump cuts with spoken dialogue
- Native lip-sync and authentic energy
- MODUS branding and tagline
- Platform-optimized format

## Video Structure

1. **Hook** (2-3s): Grab attention with problem or curiosity
2. **Setup** (3-4s): Explain the context or pain point
3. **Reveal** (3-4s): Introduce MODUS as the solution
4. **Benefit** (3-4s): Show what makes it different
5. **CTA** (2-3s): What should they do next?

## Tips for Maximum Impact

- **Relatability**: Your authentic voice beats perfect production
- **Platform Native**: Use trending sounds, hashtags, trends
- **Specificity**: "AI OS that tracks your life" beats generic "AI tool"
- **Controversy**: Lean into the chatbot critique (your positioning)
- **Visual Variety**: Mix UI demos, animations, text overlays
- **MODUS Purple**: Let it breathe — don't overuse, let it accent key moments

## Best Platforms

- **TikTok**: High-velocity, trend-focused, creator-authentic content performs best
- **Instagram Reels**: Professional with personality
- **YouTube Shorts**: Educational positioning works well

Create multiple variations targeting different audiences:
- Founders/builders
- Remote workers
- Productivity enthusiasts
- AI-first professionals

Each variation can highlight different MODUS capabilities while maintaining consistent branding.
