# MODUS Podcast Video Creator

Generate engaging two-host podcast videos (60+ seconds) perfect for team communications, educational content, thought leadership, and internal knowledge sharing. Features dynamic multi-shot dialogue with native lip-sync.

## Brand Guidelines for Your Video

- **Primary Color**: #7C3AED (MODUS violet)
- **Style**: Professional, conversational, authentic
- **Duration**: 60+ seconds (flexible)
- **Format**: 1080p video with native audio
- **Key Message**: Thought leadership on AI, operating systems, and intelligence
- **Tone**: Knowledgeable peers, engaging discussion

## How to Use

1. **Plan Your Topic**:
   - **Internal discussions**: Team learning, company updates, culture
   - **Thought leadership**: AI/OS/future of work themes
   - **Educational**: Explainer conversations between experts
   - **Product insights**: Deep dives on MODUS capabilities
   - **Interview-style**: Q&A with guests or team members

2. **Write Your Dialogue**:
   - 2 hosts, natural conversation flow
   - 60-90 seconds = ~300-400 words per host
   - Structure: Hook → Topic Setup → Discussion → Conclusion
   
   **Example Structure**:
   - **Host A**: "So everyone's building AI now, but..."
   - **Host B**: "But nothing's actually personal, right?"
   - **Host A**: "Exactly. That's why we built MODUS as an OS..."
   - **Host B**: "What makes an OS different from a chatbot?"
   - **Host A**: "It learns, it connects, it runs for you..."
   - **Host B**: "Check out MODUS Pilot to see what we mean"

3. **Host Setup**:
   - Choose your two hosts (team members, founders, experts)
   - Optional: Voice cloning for Host A (more polished feel)
   - Natural, authentic conversational flow

## Output

A professional 1080p video with:
- Multi-shot dynamic camera work
- Two hosts in natural conversation
- Native audio with lip-sync
- MODUS branding and color accents
- Professional lighting and framing
- Optional captions (skipped by default)

## Script Framework

**2-Host Discussion: "Operating Systems vs. Chatbots"**

*Host A*: "Everyone's obsessed with better AI models, right? But I think we're asking the wrong question."

*Host B*: "Yeah, I feel like we're just chasing bigger numbers."

*Host A*: "Exactly. We built MODUS on a different premise: what if AI was part of your operating system instead of just a tool you talk to?"

*Host B*: "So like, instead of opening a chatbot, it just... knows what you're doing?"

*Host A*: "Right. It tracks your email, learns your calendar, knows your habits. It's not waiting for you to ask — it's running for you."

*Host B*: "That's the difference between an OS and a chatbot. One runs, one responds."

*Host A*: "Exactly. And that's what MODUS is. Your personal AI operating system."

*Host B*: "Wild. People should check it out at [yoursite.com]."

## Tips for Maximum Impact

- **Natural Flow**: Conversations should feel like peers, not scripted
- **Interruptions**: Real podcasts have overlap — it's okay to interrupt thoughtfully
- **Energy**: Match enthusiasm and build on ideas
- **Specificity**: Use examples, not abstractions
- **Purple Presence**: Let your brand color accent key moments (logos, graphics)
- **Audio Quality**: Native audio is the deliverable — captions add noise
- **Length**: 60-90s for social, longer for YouTube/internal use

## Where to Use

- **Internal comms**: Slack channel, team meetings, onboarding
- **YouTube**: Episodic thought leadership content
- **LinkedIn**: Professional community engagement
- **Email**: Newsletter-style educational series
- **Social media**: Clip-able moments and discussions
