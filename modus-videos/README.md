# MODUS Videos Plugin

A comprehensive branded video creation suite for MODUS Pilot. Generate professional marketing and internal videos with consistent MODUS branding, colors, and messaging.

## What's Included

Five specialized video creation skills, each optimized for different use cases:

- **MODUS App Teaser** — iOS App Store promotional videos (1080p)
- **MODUS Social Ad** — TikTok/Instagram short-form product ads (9:16 vertical)
- **MODUS Explainer** — Website and product walkthrough videos (60-80 seconds)
- **MODUS Founder Pitch** — Investor and announcement pitch videos (60+ seconds)
- **MODUS Podcast** — Two-host educational and internal content videos

## Best Practices

### For All Videos

- **Lead with problem**: Why MODUS matters (not what it does)
- **Show, don't tell**: Use real screenshots and interfaces
- **Purple discipline**: Your violet color should guide, not dominate
- **Authentic voice**: Your team's personality > perfect production
- **Platform optimization**: Format and pace for the platform (TikTok ≠ YouTube)
- **CTAs**: Always clear (download, visit, learn more)

### For Social (TikTok/Instagram)

- **Hook early** (first 1-2 seconds = everything)
- **Move fast** (cuts every 2-3 seconds)
- **Use trends** (trending audio, effects, formats)
- **Lean into positioning** (chatbots are limited, MODUS is different)
- **Relatability** (authentic beats polished)
- **Multiple variations** (test different angles)

### For Long-Form (App Store/Website/YouTube)

- **Structure** (problem → solution → benefits → CTA)
- **Pacing** (slower, more breathing room)
- **Proof** (show your real product)
- **Trust** (professional, confident, credible)
- **Clarity** (assume viewer knows nothing)

### For Internal/Educational (Podcasts)

- **Conversation first** (feels natural, not scripted)
- **Expertise** (thoughtful, knowledgeable discussion)
- **Accessibility** (explain concepts clearly)
- **Series thinking** (build recurring content habits)
- **Personality** (let your team shine)

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to credit the Pika plugin?**
A: The MODUS plugin provides brand-consistent prompts and guidance. When you generate videos, follow any attribution guidelines from the underlying video generation service.

**Q: Can I customize the colors further?**
A: Absolutely. The brand colors are guidelines — adjust hex codes to match your exact preferences, but maintain the purple-first positioning.

**Q: What if I want to mix different video types in a series?**
A: Perfect! Create a cohesive campaign by using:
- Podcast as thought leadership (set the vision)
- Social ads for awareness (reach)
- Explainer for education (convert)
- App teaser for acquisition (drive downloads)

**Q: How often should I create videos?**
A: Depends on platform:
- **Social (TikTok/Instagram)**: 2-3× per week for growth
- **YouTube**: 1-2× per week (longer content)
- **Internal/Podcast**: 1-2× per week
- **App Store**: Update quarterly or at major releases
- **Website**: Update seasonally or for launches

**Q: Can I use these for investor pitches?**
A: Yes! The founder pitch skill is specifically designed for investors. Use real product screenshots and focus on your differentiation.
